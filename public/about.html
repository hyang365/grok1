About TripTable

TripTable shows where Yale students travel and study abroad, rated by students themselves. Browse the map, compare places and programs, plan trips with friends, and find somewhere that fits your budget.

How ratings work

Every rating and review is anonymous. Nobody, including other students, can see who posted one. A country's score averages the ratings for the country and all of its cities, and the map's shading follows that score. Signing in requires a Yale Google account, so everything comes from Yale students.

Plans and friends

When you save a trip, it adds to the anonymous "planning" counts everyone sees. Only friends you've accepted can see your actual trips and dates. Add friends by their Yale email from the Friends page.

Getting there

Flight links open Google Flights for real prices. The trip finder's costs are rough estimates for planning, not quotes. Bus and train times come from CTtransit, Metro-North and Amtrak.

Before you travel

Travel guidance on TripTable is a summary. Check travel.state.gov and Yale's own travel policies before you book, especially for Yale-funded or for-credit trips.

Questions or problems

Use Send feedback in the profile menu on TripTable. It goes straight to the team.
