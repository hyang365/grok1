Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

TripTable is a student-built travel planner for the Yale community. It is not an official Yale University service. This page explains what TripTable collects, why, and what you control.

What we collect

  • Your account email. When you sign in with your Yale Google account or an emailed link, we receive your @yale.edu email address. It is used only to confirm you are a Yale student and to sign you in.
  • Reviews you post. Your ratings and text, the term you went, and the college and class year you choose. Reviews are public on TripTable but never show your name or email.
  • Trips you save while signed in. The place, season and dates. Only you can see your trips. Other students see only anonymous totals, such as "12 planning" or "3 overlap your dates", never who.
  • Reports. If you report a review, we store your report so it can be moderated.

Without signing in, your trips, bucket list, friends list and settings are saved only in your own browser and never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.
  • We don't show your name, email or NetID to other students.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, TripTable requests only your basic profile and email address. TripTable's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores accounts, reviews and saved trips.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google Flights and transit providers (CTtransit, MTA, Amtrak via Amtraker) are linked or queried for travel information. Opening a Google Flights link takes you to Google, under Google's own policies.

Your choices

  • Delete any of your reviews or trips at any time from TripTable.
  • Download your saved trips and bucket list from Profile → Settings.
  • To delete your account and everything tied to it, email yuvin.kang@yale.edu.

Contact

Questions about privacy: yuvin.kang@yale.edu.
